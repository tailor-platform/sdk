After a command finishes in an interactive terminal, `tailor` now lists your unread WARNING and CRITICAL platform notifications briefly on stderr, with pointers to `tailor notification list` and `tailor notification read <id>`. A CRITICAL notification is listed ahead of newer WARNINGs. It is best effort: the lookup runs alongside the command, and if it has not answered when the command ends, `tailor` waits at most 150 ms more before dropping it. It never changes a command's exit code, and it marks nothing read. It keeps appearing after each command until you read the notifications with `tailor notification read` or dismiss them. After a failed lookup it pauses for 5 minutes, so a platform outage does not cost a request per command. It is never shown in CI, under `--json`, in a pipe, or after help output. It is also skipped after `login`, `logout`, `auth`, `api`, `query` and the `notification` commands themselves, and after commands that only work locally: `init`, `generate`, `show`, `plugin`, `skills`, `completion`, `crashreport`, `upgrade`, `profile`, `open`, and `user` other than `user pat`. Set `TAILOR_NOTIFICATION_BANNER=off` to turn it off.
