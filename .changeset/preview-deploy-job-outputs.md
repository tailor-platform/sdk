Workflows generated by `tailor setup ci preview` now expose the per-PR workspace from the `tailor-preview-deploy` job as the outputs `workspace-id`, `workspace-name`, and `app-url`. A job of your own can use `needs: tailor-preview-deploy` to run tests or deploy extra assets against the preview workspace. `tailor setup check` reports the template as outdated; re-run `tailor setup ci preview` to pick it up. If you group apps with a comma in `tailor setup ci coordinate --action`, re-run `tailor setup ci action --force` for each grouped app before re-running `setup ci coordinate`.
